CampusHub v1 — How It Works
Nov 5, 2026 · @Sam
Overview
Version 1 of CampusHub shows one complete flow: a committee posts an event, the coordinator and dean approve it, students apply, and the committee accepts or rejects them by looking at their profiles. The first goal is to demo this to the dean.
Included in version 1
Login with roles: student, faculty and dean
Profiles and search
Event posting with approval by the tagged coordinator and the dean
Applying to events and tracking applications
Basic points and tiers
Left for version 2: certificate wallet, ranking boards, stream sections, notifications and reminders.
Complete system flow

complete version 1 system flow · three lanes
Everyone enters through login or registration and lands on the home screen. Then each role follows its own lane: the committee posts an event, the coordinator and dean approve it, students apply, and the committee's decision updates the student's points, tier and profile. The sections below explain each part in detail.
Roles
Every account is a student, a faculty member or the dean. The coordinator is not a separate account type: it is a faculty member tagged on one event.

Event coordinator (faculty tagged on an event)
Read the event post and approve or reject it
Faculty
Edit own profile, be tagged as coordinator. No phone number field
Dean (super admin)
Approve or reject every event, view everything. No phone number field
Opening the app: login and registration

opening the app: login and registration · 3 decisions

Privacy rules, points and tiers
Contact privacy
Phone numbers never show in search or on student profiles.
A committee sees a phone number only for students who applied to its own event.
Faculty and dean profiles have no phone number field. Students can reach them by email only.
Points and tiers
The committee sets the points for an event when it creates the post.
When a student is accepted, those points are added to their profile and their tier updates.
Tier rules are still open: the brief gives two different mappings of year of study to tier, and the team needs to pick one before this part is built.
