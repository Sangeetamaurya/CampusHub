===== PAGE 9 =====
A committee student is a normal student whose profile lists their committee, so everything in the student flow also applies to them. The extra tools let them create an event, tag the coordinator and send it for approval. If an approver says no, the post comes back with a reason to fix and resubmit. Once it is live, they get the list of applicants, review each profile, and accept or reject. The student is notified either way.  committee student flow · post, approve, review, accept  CampusHub v1 — How It Works Page 9 of 18

===== PAGE 10 =====
Faculty  A faculty member gets a notification when a committee tags them as coordinator. The event waits under Pending approvals, and they read it and approve or reject. A rejection sends the reason back to the committee. An approval moves the event on to the dean. Faculty do not apply to events and have no phone number on their profile.  faculty flow · review tagged events  CampusHub v1 — How It Works Page 10 of 18

===== PAGE 11 =====
Dean  The dean is the super admin. Every event comes to the dean under Pending approvals, and on approval it goes live in the feed and the committee is notified. A rejection sends the reason back to the committee. The dean can also open any event, profile or application in the system.  dean flow · final approval and full access  CampusHub v1 — How It Works Page 11 of 18

===== PAGE 12 =====
Roles  Every account is a student, a faculty member or the dean. The coordinator is not a separate account type: it is a faculty member tagged on one event. A committee member is not a separate account type either: it is a normal student whose profile lists their committee.  Role   What they can do  Student   Edit own profile, search and view profiles, apply to events open to their tier, track their applications  Committee student (profile lists the committee)  Create event posts, tag the coordinator, review applicants' profiles, accept or reject applications, see the phone number of students who applied to their event  Event coordinator (faculty tagged on an event)  Read the event post and approve or reject it  Faculty   Edit own profile, be tagged as coordinator. No phone number field  Dean (super admin)   Approve or reject every event, view everything. No phone number field  CampusHub v1 — How It Works Page 12 of 18

===== PAGE 17 =====
Privacy rules, points and tiers  Contact privacy  Phone numbers never show in search or on student profiles.  A committee sees a phone number only for students who applied to its own event.  Faculty and dean profiles have no phone number field. Students can reach them by email only.  Points and tiers  The committee sets the points for an event when it creates the post.  When a student is accepted, those points are added to their profile and their tier updates.  Tier rules are still open: the brief gives two different mappings of year of study to tier, and the team needs to pick one before this part is built.  Build order and open questions  Suggested build order  1.   Login with student, faculty and dean accounts  2.   Profiles and search  3.   Create event, tag the coordinator, and the Pending approvals screen  4.   Feed and the apply form  5.   My applications and the Applicants screen with Accept or Reject  6.   Points and tiers  7.   Demo data: a dean, a few faculty, about ten students with filled profiles, and events at different approval stages  Screen   Who sees it   What it shows  Profile   Everyone   Picture, about, certificates, projects, experience, event contributions, and tier and points for students  Search   Everyone   Find any student, faculty member or the dean and open their profile  CampusHub v1 — How It Works Page 17 of 18
